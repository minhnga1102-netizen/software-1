==HOW TO PLAY==

Type one of these commands:

POKEDEX   - view the Pokemon you have caught
CATCH     - try to catch a wild Pokemon
ITEM      - collect the item in the current room
INVENTORY - view your items
MOVE      - move to another room
SAVE      - save your game
LOPETA    - quit the game

==HOW TO WIN (choose 1 route)==
Route 1 - Legend Hunter: catch Mewtwo and collect all 3 balls.
Route 2 - Master Pokedex: catch all 4 Pokemon.
Route 3 - Explorer: go to the Pokemon League with at least 2 balls and 2 Pokemon.
